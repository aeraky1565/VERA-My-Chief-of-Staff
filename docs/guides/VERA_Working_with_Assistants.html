Who does what, step by step

VERA works alongside ARIA (flights), WILLA (money plans), TESS (job search) and PIES (work); HAILEY (home search) runs on its own. This guide follows each job from start to finish and shows whose turn it is at every step: YOUVERAARIAWILLATESSPIES. How the links are built is in the System Documentation, §21.

✈️Plan and book a trip, from idea to booked

Travel › Plan › Trip Ideas. Victoria can follow steps 6 to 8 on her own.

  1. YOUAdd the idea and its options. 📋 Copy for an AI chat gives a chat everything it needs; paste its answer back as options.
  2. YOUWILLACan we afford it? On an option, ↗ Hand off › Copy for WILLA, paste it to WILLA, then bring the answer back with 💰 Budget from WILLA.
  3. YOUMark the idea Shortlisted, star (★) the option you like and tap 🧭 Scout. Up to three options can be scouted at once.
  4. ARIAVERANext morning ARIA prices the idea's months; VERA shows the cheapest dates on the option. One morning.
  5. YOUPick the dates: 🏁 Settle this trip downloads a calendar event. Add it to the shared calendar (all-day, with "Trip" in the title).
  6. VERAARIAWithin the hour VERA links the idea to the trip and turns the scout into a watch. ARIA keeps the fares it found and watches from then on: weekly from 6 months out, every 3 days from 3 months, daily in the last 6 weeks.
  7. ARIAYOU✈️ Book now arrives (flag, Slack and ARIA's email): the fare is under your ceiling and near the lowest ARIA has seen. Check it's still live and book on the airline's site. Your confirmation email does the rest.

    VERA sees the flights in the itinerary and tells ARIA "booked"; ARIA stops pricing the next morning.

  8. YOUWILLAOnce everything is booked, the flag "Send the final total to WILLA" shows what was spent. Send it with the WILLA line on the option.

Moving the trip on the calendar is fine at any step: VERA updates ARIA's request, and ARIA raises 📅 Trip dates changed so you can check its dates.

📅A trip that's already on the calendar

  1. ARIAARIA adds every calendar trip to its list as a Draft. A Draft isn't priced yet.
  2. YOUTo have its flights watched: Travel › My Trips › the trip › Ask ARIA. VERA fills in airports, travellers and a price ceiling from the trip budget; check them and send.
  3. ARIAFrom the next morning it watches. Low flags such as 🛫 Add airports or 💲 Set a price ceiling mean ARIA needs that before it can price. You can also fill the yellow cells in ARIA's Watchlist yourself; ARIA never overwrites them.
  4. ARIAYOU👀 Set up a SlickTrip watch: ARIA never creates price watches itself. Create one in SlickTrip for the trip and paste its Watch ID into the trip's row in ARIA's Watchlist. Until then ARIA still prices it by search.
  5. YOUSame as above from here: Book now → book → the confirmation email closes the loop. Then 🛑 Stop the SlickTrip watch asks you to stop that watch in SlickTrip. Driving? ARIA skips the trip.

💼Job search with TESS

  1. YOUSet what you want once: Career › North Star (level, pay floors, work mode, where, deal-breakers). VERA saves it straight into TESS's settings.
  2. TESSWeekday mornings: matches from the LinkedIn alerts, scored, with a tailored resume, cover letter and fit note, and a digest email.
  3. YOUTap Triage in VERA in the digest. In Career › Agents › Pipeline set each one's status: Applied, Passed, Ignored… VERA writes it back to TESS's Tracker.
  4. YOUTESSTeach TESS: it learns from Saved, Applied, Interviewing and Passed with a Pass reason (Too senior, Location, Pay…). VERA can't set the Pass reason yet, so add it in TESS's Tracker. Three alike and TESS starts adjusting scores.
  5. YOUApply yourself. TESS never applies for you.
  6. TESSYOUOn the 1st and 15th the calibration review emails up to 5 suggested changes. Reply in your Claude chat to approve any.
  7. YOUFound a role somewhere else? Career › Agents › Exchange › Copy MATCHER-CONTEXT, paste it with the job description into a Claude chat, and paste the ROLE-MATCH answer back into Exchange.

Each month VERA also sends your new evidence to the Wins bank doc that TESS writes from. Nothing to do.

🗂️Work wins from PIES

PIES stays on the work machine. The only bridge is a copy and paste you do yourself.

  1. PIESMonthly: writes the PIES-BRIEF (your accomplishments, sanitised).
  2. YOUCopy it and paste it into Career › Agents › Exchange. Check the preview: names, figures and project names are marked. Then import.
  3. VERAIt lands in Evidence and readiness.
  4. YOUEach quarter: Exchange › Copy VERA-INTENTS, and paste it into the "Career Intents" section of the PIES Goals doc.

💰Money with WILLA

  1. WILLAVERAKeep the planner up to date in WILLA. VERA reads it live for Finances › Net worth, Goals and the Summary, and never changes it.
  2. VERAA flag 30 days before a CD matures, so you can decide in time.
  3. YOUTrip budgets go through WILLA by copy and paste (steps 2 and 8 of planning a trip).

🏠Home search with HAILEY

HAILEY works on its own: nothing goes between it and VERA. Change markets, budgets and alert lines in HAILEY's own sheet; HAILEY emails you an alert when a home crosses one, and a digest every Friday.
