The act-now test: all three, or it goes elsewhere
1It expires. A price, a spot or a deadline that is gone in hours or a few days, and the message says when. 2Only you can decide. Book, apply, keep or pass, not a reminder to do a chore. 3One tap moves it forward. Every message has a main action, a later and a Pass. Pass teaches the assistant.
Who posts here
ARIABook now, a price drop after you booked (rebook or claim the difference), award seats that opened. HAILEYA new listing that scores very high, a price cut or a status change on a saved home, an open house for a saved home. TESSA strong match posted in the last few hours. Its daily finds stay in its own digest. VERAIts own deadlines that need a decision: free cancellation ending, a trip decision due, a refund or return window closing. PIESNot visible to VERA, so it doesn't post here.
Stays out of #vera-act-now
A flight delay or a bill due today (urgent, but no decision: #vera-notifications) · HAILEY's Friday digest and TESS's daily list (reading: email) · agent run problems (#vera-logs and Agent health).
How the channel stays useful
• Each assistant has a daily cap (Config act_now_cap_aria, …_hailey, …_tess). Past it, items wait for the morning email.
• One message per item. Price moves and status changes come as replies under it, not new posts.
• When it expires or you decide, VERA rewrites the message (Expired, Booked ✓, Saved ✓), so the channel only shows what's still open.
• Quiet hours apply, except when the deadline falls before morning.
• Default delivery is Both: Slack plus a VERA app push in its own high-priority “Act now” notification channel on Android.
Can the installed VERA app push?
Yes. The dashboard you install from the browser can show its own notifications, with the VERA icon, even when the app is closed.
iPhoneiOS 16.4 or later, and only once VERA is added to the Home Screen. You tap Turn on notifications once inside VERA and allow it. The notification shows a title and text; there are no buttons, and tapping it opens the right screen in VERA. AndroidWorks from Chrome once installed. It can show up to two buttons, such as Mark paid and Snooze. ComputerChrome, Edge and Safari can show them too, if you want them there.
What it needs on VERA's side: the app signs up each phone once, and the nightly and hourly jobs send through Google's free push service (Firebase Cloud Messaging), using a key kept in Script Properties. Nothing else to install.
Slack or app?
Each kind of notification can go to Slack, the VERA app, or both (Settings). Slack keeps the full message, its buttons and its history in #vera-notifications; the app push is the shorter, lock-screen version that opens VERA.
