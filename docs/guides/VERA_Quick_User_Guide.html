🌙How VERA works for you

VERA works in the background. Most days you only need the morning email and a minute with your flags.

The daily rhythm

  • 11 PM: the nightly run reads your calendars, tasks, finances, PTO, travel, health, home and more, and decides what you need to know.
  • 7 AM: the morning briefing email with today's priorities and new flags. No email means nothing needs you.
  • During the day: reminders, flight alerts and travel messages arrive when they matter, not on a schedule.
  • Wednesday 8 AM: the Weekend Planner, early enough for the weekend forecast.
  • 1st of the month: the Monthly Life Review email.

What to do with a flag

  • High: needs you soon. Medium: this week. Low: good to know, often an opportunity.
  • Resolve when it's done. Snooze to see it again on a date. Acknowledge only says "seen", and the flag stays open.
  • Ignored for 3 days, a flag goes up one level of urgency. After 7 days it's marked stale and set to High.
  • VERA learns: flags you keep snoozing or ignoring get suppressed unless things genuinely get worse.

Flags you might see

  • High · Travel: "Your trip is in 3 days and packing is 40% done."
  • High · Pattern: "Heavy week, gym streak broken, no clear weekend: burnout building."
  • Medium · Finance: "Dining out is up 66% on last month."
  • Medium · PTO: "3 of 20 vacation days used; you're behind pace."
  • Low · Interests: "You noted jazz; there's a jazz festival in 5 days."

Slack channels

  • #vera-chat: talk to VERA, same as the dashboard Chat.
  • #vera-notifications: the alerts worth seeing (flags, reminders, travel).
  • #vera-logs: what VERA did overnight and any errors; check it when something seems off.
  • Change where each message goes in System › Settings › Notifications.

🏠Today & planning

What needs you today. VERA runs every night: it reads your calendars, tasks, email and sheets, raises flags, and sends the morning briefing.

Home

🏠 Home

The day at a glance: the ARIA banner when a fare is worth booking, the travel card when a trip is close, flag and task counts, shopping, PTO and the Home slot card, which shows whichever area needs you most right now.

  • Log a grocery run tile: snap a receipt from anywhere (see Finances › Groceries).

Flags

🚩 Flags

What VERA thinks needs attention, with urgency High, Medium or Low. Acknowledge, snooze or resolve each one. Resolved flags leave the list; snoozed ones come back on their date.

  • Easy mode: when there's too much at once, VERA defers non-critical flags for a few days and says so on Home.

Tasks

✅ Tasks

VERA tasks and Google Tasks together. Add, complete, edit due dates and recurrence. Tasks untouched for a week get a gentle flag.

Projects

🏗️ Projects

Multi-step projects. Describe what it's for and VERA drafts the checklist (asking a few questions first if needed). Shown only while a project has open tasks.

Chat

💬 Chat

Ask anything or tell VERA to do something: complete a task, add a bill, plan meals, add an itinerary item, log a purchase. The same chat answers in Slack #vera-chat. See the cheat sheet for examples.

PTO Planner

🌴 PTO Planner

Vacation and personal-time balances for both of you, pace against the year, and suggested clear windows. Balances come from Config › PTO.

✈️Travel

Four groups of tiles: Plan (Trip Ideas, ARIA), Trips (My Trips, Itinerary), Places (Countries, Bucket List) and Travelers (Profiles, Our bags, Gifts). Every trip has a permanent ID, so moving its dates on the calendar never loses its itinerary, packing or emails.

My Trips New

Travel › Trips › My Trips

Every trip from your calendars on a 15-month strip, pencilled-in trips, and past trips. Tap a trip for its summary: a pill per day (red = a night with nowhere to sleep, amber = a tight transfer or open decision), what's booked, and what's left.

  • Readiness per traveller: documents, member numbers on bookings, seats, lounge access, prescriptions to refill.
  • Who's travelling: pick people from Profiles.

Itinerary

Travel › Trips › My Itinerary

Flights, stays, transfers and plans per day, with live flight status on travel days. Open options (two dinners for one night) show as decisions to make.

  • Staying with family? Add an arranged stay so those nights count as covered.

Trip emails New

Trip summary · System › Trip emails

Who gets each trip's Travel Day email. You always do. Victoria and other travellers only when switched on for that trip; you can also add any address. Victoria's solo trips send none.

Packing New

Trip summary › Packing
  1. Start: VERA builds the list from weather, trip type, nights, laundry and your profile; add staples you always pack, or copy from a similar past trip.
  2. Pack: tap an item to move it To buy → Ready → In the bag, with quantities and who brings it; outfits per day; Pack a bag sorts items into carry-on / checked, with the luggage advice at the top.
  3. Before you go: a Buy list for what's missing, a checkout tick-off at the hotel, and a short review afterwards that teaches next time.

Luggage & Our bags New

Travel › Travelers › Our bags

List your bags once (size, carry-on or checked, empty weight). Each trip then suggests which bags to take per person, about-weights against the airline limit, what's free on your fare and which status or card waives the checked bag. Liquids and fragile gifts go checked; meds and chargers go carry-on.

Before you leave home New

Home Front › House › Leaving home

A checklist per home that adapts to season, length and who's leaving (AC and plants in summer, pipes in winter, mail on long trips). It appears in the trip summary; either of you can tick. Reminders come in the night-before email and on the morning you leave. Use Heading out for time away that isn't a trip.

Traveller profiles New

Travel › Travelers › Profiles

Documents (passports, visas, ETAs), Global Entry / PreCheck, airline and hotel memberships, seat and hotel preferences, diet and allergies, medications. Only the last 4 digits of any number are kept. VERA reminds you before a passport or Global Entry runs out ahead of a trip.

Gifts New

Travel › Travelers › Gifts

Gifts per trip: who for, kind, budget, status (to buy → bought → packed), what you gave them before, ideas for the destination, and customs notes for getting it home.

Countries & Bucket List New

Travel › Places

Countries as a map, list or timeline, with occasions and trip notes. The Bucket List holds dream places with things to do, best months, vacation days a trip would take, points needed for two seats, and a long-weekend filter (direct flights under 7 h). Plan several places as one Trip Idea.

Trip Ideas & ARIA

Travel › Plan

Trip Ideas: trips with no dates yet, a loose brief and the shapes the trip could take. ARIA: your travel agent's fare watch, verdicts and what needs a look. Ask ARIA to watch a trip from its card.

Emails per trip: 48 hours before (pre-trip briefing), the night before (with the leaving-home list), each travel day, and after you're home (what to remember).

💰Finances

Four groups: Overview (Summary), Day to day (Budget, Bills & cashflow, Groceries, Phone plan), Long term (Net worth, Goals, Planned Purchases, FSA & HSA) and Paperwork (Accounts, Cards, Contracts, Tax).

Summary & check-in New

Finances › Overview › Summary

The quick glance: net worth, cash, goals set aside, plan status. Before each quarter, the money check-in lists what to discuss and the decisions coming due; record decisions and owners there.

Budget & What-if New

Finances › Day to day › Budget

VERA works out the month: plan vs actual by category from your transactions. What if tries a change (a new bill, a raise) and shows the effect before you commit.

Bills & cashflow New

Finances › Day to day › Bills & cashflow

Every bill with its due day, account, autopay and card, plus the joint account timeline: what lands when, and whether the balance stays above the floor.

Groceries & Log a grocery run New

Finances › Day to day › Groceries
  1. Tap Log a grocery run (also on Home and in Life › Quick capture) and snap the receipt.
  2. VERA reads the store, date, every line and the total. Choose what goes to the pantry; items on any shopping list are ticked off.
  3. Save: the pantry, the shopping lists and the month's grocery spend update together.

Phone plan

Finances › Day to day › Phone plan

Who pays what on the family plan, paybacks received, and the monthly total.

Goals, Net worth, Planned Purchases

Finances › Long term

Goals and Net worth are read live from your planner sheet. Planned Purchases take a big buy from first idea to bought: must-haves, options compared, money from a savings goal, and what happens to the old item.

Cards, perks & paperwork

Finances › Paperwork

Card rewards and perks (with expiry flags), loyalty points, accounts, contracts with notice periods, and the tax document checklist.

🏡Home & food

Shopping lists, and Home Front's five groups: House, Chores, Kitchen, Hosting and Savings.

Shopping

🛒 Shopping

One list per store. Done shopping at the store logs the trip; with a receipt VERA fills the pantry too.

House

Home Front › House

Household info, home items with warranties and service dates, vehicles (oil, registration, insurance), neighbourhood and HOA notices, and the leaving-home checklists.

Chores

Home Front › Chores

Chores by cadence, reset automatically, and chores for others with rewards.

Kitchen

Home Front › Kitchen

Meal plan for the week (VERA can suggest one), recipes, the pantry (what's on hand, what to use soon, restock predictions) and takeout favourites.

Hosting & Savings

Home Front › Hosting / Savings

House guests, gatherings (guests, menu, to-dos, budget) and the home guide; coupons and the resell list.

🩺Health, family & people

Body & care

Health › Body / Care

Fitness and the gym check-in after each scheduled workout, the morning routine, prescriptions with refill dates, and appointments.

Family Planning

Health › Family › Family Planning

One shared plan for leave, money, health, childcare and paperwork, by phase. Victoria sees the same plan on her dashboard.

Cycle tracker New

Family Planning › Cycle

Tap Period started (today or another date) and Period ended. The card shows the day of the cycle, how that day usually feels, the next period, and lateness. In the Trying phase it also shows the fertile window and peak days. Reminders go to a private channel you choose in System › Notifications.

People

👥 People

The address book, important dates with lead times, gift ideas per person and wish lists.

💼Career

Five groups: Direction (North Star, Readiness), Proof (Evidence, Credentials), Search (Brand, Market), Agents (Pipeline, Exchange) and Network.

Credentials New

Career › Proof › Credentials

Certifications and degrees with expiry, renewal credits needed and earned. Reminders at 90, 30 and 7 days, and when credits fall behind pace. Shared with the job agent.

Direction, proof & search

Career

The career ladder and readiness per track, evidence tagged to criteria, the job agent's matches, your network with warmth and last touch, and the market view of your package.

🌿Life New

Quick capture

Life › Quick capture

Type it once and VERA files it: "Did 30 min of Spanish", "that ramen place", "gave $50 to…". Undo if it guessed wrong. The Receipt chip logs a grocery run.

Coming up

Life › Coming up

The next 60 days of weddings, shows, parties and dinners, with what to do before each (gift, outfit, RSVP) and a leave-by time.

Growth

Life › Growth

Now, Goals, Habits, Learning (books and courses), Trials (small experiments) and Review. A weekly nudge when a Now goal has had no practice.

Explore

Life › Explore

Places, trip lessons, things we love, ideas, giving and notes.

🛠System & the sheet

System tab

🛠 System

Reference (assistants, documents), Checks (test sends, API health) and Settings (notifications, trip emails, config, feature flags).

Notifications

System › Settings › Notifications

Turn each message on or off and choose where it goes: email, #vera-notifications, #vera-chat or #vera-logs.

The Config tab New

VERA sheet › CONFIG

Every setting, grouped: system-wide first (general, notifications, calendars, linked files, assistants), then one section per feature. Column B is the value VERA reads; C says what it does; D links to the tab it affects. Collapse sections with the − / + on the left. Grey italic rows show VERA's defaults: change the value to override.

Colour-coded sheet tabs New

VERA sheet

Tabs sit together by area and share the dashboard colours: Today Travel Finances Home Health & family Career Life VERA internal (last). Never move columns in a data tab: VERA reads them by position.
