VERA in 5 minutes

VERA reads your calendar every night and tells you what needs you. You don't need the dashboard. Just write calendar events the way VERA understands, read the morning email, and ask when you need something.

1When VERA does things

11 PMReads your calendars, tasks, bills and trips. Decides what matters. 7 AMSends the morning email. No email means nothing needs you. 🎉 All dayReminders and flight alerts in Slack, only when they matter. WednesdayWeekend ideas, once the weekend weather is known. The 1stA short monthly review email. TripsAn email 2 days before, the night before, on each travel day, and when you're home.

2Write your calendar so VERA understands

VERA reads the words in the title. Use these and it does the rest.

✈️A trip
Trip: Chicago for Christmas
An all-day event, 2 days or longer, with Trip, Vacation, Cruise, Getaway, Visit, Tour, Travel or Holiday in the title. Put it on your shared calendar. Moving the dates later is fine: VERA keeps everything attached. Once it's on the calendar, ARIA can watch its flights.
🌴A day off from work
Vacation PTO
On your work calendar: an all-day event called Vacation uses a vacation day; all-day PTO uses a personal day; a timed "PTO" uses just those hours.
🩺A doctor or dentist visit
DR: Victoria - Dentist - Dr. Patel
Start with DR:, then the person, the type of visit, and the doctor if you like. VERA remembers the last visit and reminds you when the next is due.
🏋️A workout
Gym (notes: EXERCISE)
Type EXERCISE in the event's notes. Afterwards VERA asks "did you go?" so it can track your streak.
🎂A birthday or anniversary
Sarah's Birthday
Put it on the shared family calendar with Birthday in the title. VERA reminds you a month ahead for a gift or card.
💳A bill
Internet Bill
Put Bill, Payment, Rent or Subscription in the title on the Bills calendar. VERA warns you before it's due.
🎉A wedding, party or big night out
Emma & Tom's Wedding
Use the plain word (Wedding, Party, Concert, Dinner, Graduation…). VERA tells you what to do ahead: weddings 2 months out, concerts 3 weeks, parties 2 weeks, dinners a week.
🛏️Guests staying over
Guest Stay: Mom & Dad
Use Guest Stay or Guest Visit in the title, so VERA can help you get the house ready.

3When VERA flags something

A flag is VERA saying "this needs you". Each has a level:

High: soonMedium: this weekLow: good to know
✅ ResolveDone. It goes away.
😴 SnoozeNot now. It comes back on the day you pick.
👀 AcknowledgeOnly means "seen". It stays open.

✈️ Book now comes from ARIA, VERA's travel helper, which watches flight prices for your trips. It means the fare is good: book it yourself, then add it to the trip. ARIA watches flights only, not hotels.

Ignore a flag for 3 days and it gets more urgent. Keep ignoring a kind of flag and VERA learns to stop sending it.

4Ask VERA anything

Message #vera-chat in Slack (or the Chat in the dashboard). Plain words, no commands:

"What's on this weekend?"
"Add milk to the Costco list"
"Mark the water bill paid"
"Remind me to call the plumber Friday"
"Add a dinner on the 12th at 7:30 to our trip"

5Two handy extras

🧾 Snap a grocery receipt. Tap Log a grocery run and take a photo. VERA fills the pantry, ticks off the shopping list and counts the spending.

🚪 Leaving home? Tap Heading out for a checklist (lights, thermostat, plants) that fits the season and how long you'll be away.

6If something looks wrong

Check the event title has the right word (step 2). Still odd? Look in #vera-logs in Slack, or tell VERA in #vera-chat what you expected.

Changes to the calendar show up after the next 11 PM run.
