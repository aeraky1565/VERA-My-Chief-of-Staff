Your VERA dashboard

A short guide to the lite dashboard: what's on each tab and the few taps that matter. Tap 📖 at the top of the dashboard to open it again. Anything you tick or add here shows up on Ahmed's dashboard too, and the other way round.

🌅 Today 💬 Chat 🛒 Shopping 🏡 Home ✈️ Travel 💰 Money 👪 Family 🏗️ Projects What VERA sends you

🌅Today

Your day

What's coming up, shopping to get, chores, bills due and anything VERA wants both of you to know.

Log a grocery run

Bought groceries without the list? Tap Log a grocery run, snap the receipt, and choose what goes to the pantry. Anything on a shopping list gets ticked off, and the total counts toward this month's groceries.

💬Chat

Ask VERA

Ask a question or ask for something to be done: "What's on this weekend?", "Add milk to Costco", "When is Ahmed's next trip?", "Mark the water bill paid".

🛒Shopping

Lists

Shopping › Lists

One list per store. Add items, tick them in the store, then tap Done shopping; add the receipt and the pantry fills itself.

Card cheat sheet & Coupons

Shopping › Card cheat sheet / Coupons

Which card earns the most where, and the coupons that are still good.

🏡Home

House, chores & kitchen

Household info, chores (they reset on their own), the meal plan, recipes and what's in the pantry.

Leaving home

Home › House › Leaving home

The checklist before you go away: it changes with the season and the length of the trip. Tick items as you do them; Ahmed sees your ticks. Use Heading out for a weekend away that isn't a trip.

✈️Travel

My Trips

Travel › My Trips

Your trips, with each day's plan, packing and the leaving-home list. Packing items go To buy → Ready → In the bag with a tap.

Flights & ARIA

Travel › My Trips › a trip

ARIA is the travel helper that watches flight and hotel prices. A trip's Flights · ARIA box shows what it has found; Ask ARIA asks it to watch that trip. When you see ✈️ Book now, the fare is good: book it, or tell Ahmed. Nights with no place to sleep show 🏨 Watch this stay; 🏨 Book this stay means a good hotel price.

Your profile

Travel › Profiles

Passport and visa expiry, Global Entry / PreCheck, memberships and preferences (seat, room, diet). Only the last 4 digits of any number are kept. VERA warns before something runs out ahead of a trip.

Bucket List

Travel › Bucket List

Places you'd love to go, things to do there and the best months to visit.

Our bags & Gifts

Travel › Our bags / Gifts

Your bags (so VERA can suggest which to take) and gifts to bring for people on a trip.

💰Money

At a glance

The summary, this month's budget, bills and the joint account timeline, groceries, the phone plan, and long-term goals and net worth.

Paperwork

Accounts, cards, contracts, tax documents and FSA / HSA balances.

👪Family

Family Planning

Family › Family Planning

The shared plan: leave, money, health, childcare and paperwork. The Cycle card is at the top: tap Period started or Period ended. It shows the cycle day, the next period and, while trying, the fertile window. Only the two of you see it.

Prescriptions

Family › Prescriptions

Medications for both of you with refill dates.

Wish Lists & Address Book

Family › Wish Lists / Address Book

Everyone's wish lists, and addresses and birthdays.

🏗️Projects

Shared projects

Projects that are shared or yours, with the next step for each. Tick tasks as you finish them; adding and editing happen on Ahmed's dashboard.

✉️What VERA sends you

Travel Day email

On each travel day of a trip you're on, when Ahmed switches you on for that trip: flights, times, where you're staying and what's next.

If something looks wrong

If the dashboard doesn't load, check your connection and reload. Still stuck? Tell Ahmed.
