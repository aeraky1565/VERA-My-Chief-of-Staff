2 Understanding Your Flags
🔴 High

Needs your attention soon. Something important is overdue, approaching, or compounding. Don't sit on these.

🟡 Medium

Worth looking at this week. Not an emergency but worth addressing before it becomes one.

🟢 Low

Good to know. No urgency. Could be an opportunity, a suggestion, or a heads-up about something a few weeks out.

What to do with a flag:

✅ Acknowledge — "I've seen this"
😴 Snooze — "Remind me in a few days"
✔️ Resolve — "Done / handled"

If you ignore a flag for 3 days, it gets escalated (shown more prominently). After 7 days, escalated again. VERA learns over time — flags you consistently ignore get suppressed so they stop appearing.

6 Health Appointments — How It Works

When you schedule a doctor's appointment in Google Calendar, just add DR: to the start of the event title. VERA picks it up automatically.

Format:

DR: [Name] - [Appointment Type]
DR: [Name] - [Appointment Type] - [Doctor Name]

Examples:

DR: Ahmed - Annual Physical DR: Victoria - Dentist Cleaning DR: Ahmed - Eye Exam - Dr. Patel

VERA tracks the last visit date and flags you when you're due for the next one based on standard medical intervals.

Appointment TypeDefault Check-in
Annual PhysicalEvery 12 months
Dentist / Dental CleaningEvery 6 months
Eye Exam / OptometristEvery 12 months
DermatologyEvery 12 months
Gynecology / OB-GYNEvery 12 months
Therapy / TherapistEvery 1 month
Cardiology / EndocrinologyEvery 12 months
ChiropractorEvery 1 month
Urgent CareNever flagged — episodic

To override an interval, add interval:6 (for 6 months) anywhere in the calendar event description.

8 When to Do Nothing
✅ VERA handles these automatically — no action needed:
  • Escalating flags that sit unacknowledged for 3–7 days
  • Resetting your morning routine checklist every night
  • Syncing birthday events from your Joint Chaos calendar to Important Dates
  • Resetting household chores based on their cadence (daily / weekly / monthly)
  • Suggesting due dates for tasks you haven't dated yet
  • Refreshing all Summaries with the latest data every night
  • Suppressing flags you consistently ignore (Signal Learning)
  • Activating pacing mode when you're stretched across too many domains
  • Pausing reminders automatically when you're on vacation
  • Archiving old meal plans and seeding next week's every Saturday
  • Logging gym sessions from your calendar events

VERA runs silently on Google Apps Script. No servers, no subscriptions, no maintenance.
