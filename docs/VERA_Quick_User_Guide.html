⚑ Understanding Your Flags — what VERA is telling you, and what to do about it
🚦 Urgency Levels
High
Needs your attention soon. Will keep escalating if ignored for 3+ days.
Medium
Worth looking at this week. No emergency, but don't let it slide.
Low
Good to know. Could be an opportunity, suggestion, or heads-up.
👆 What to Do
✓  Acknowledge — "I've seen this"
💤  Snooze — "Remind me in 2 days"
✅  Resolve — "Done, handled"
Heads up: If you ignore a flag for 3 days VERA bumps it up one urgency level. After 7 days it marks it as stale.
💡 Examples of Flags You Might See
High · Travel
Your Tampa trip is in 3 days — your packing list is only 40% done.
High · Calendar
You haven't responded to a meeting invite that starts in 2 days.
High · Pattern
High workload + gym streak broken + no clear weekend — possible burnout building.
Medium · Finance
Dining out spending jumped 66% vs last month — worth a look.
Medium · PTO
You've only used 3 of 20 vacation days — you're falling behind pace.
Low · Interests
You noted interest in jazz concerts — there's a jazz festival in 5 days.
Low · Travel
Buenos Aires trip next month — Patagonia is on your bucket list and just 1.5h away.
🔁 Flag Lifecycle
1
Flag appears in your morning email
VERA generated it from the nightly analysis. Shows the source, reason, and urgency.
2
You acknowledge, snooze, or resolve
Do this from the dashboard or via button in the Slack notification. Takes one click.
3
After 3 days unacknowledged → escalated
VERA bumps the urgency level up one notch and marks it "3d". Still unread after 7 days → marked "7d stale".
4
VERA learns over time
Flags you consistently ignore get suppressed — they stop showing up unless the situation genuinely worsens.
Important: Use Resolve, not just Acknowledge, when something is actually done. Acknowledging leaves the flag open and it will keep appearing.

🌴 PTO, Travel & Health — What VERA Handles Automatically — you just live your life, VERA keeps an eye on the details
🌴 Your PTO Balance
📅
VERA Watches Your Burn-Down
VERA tracks how many vacation days you've used vs. your ideal pace. If you're falling behind it flags it automatically.
🗓
Clear Window Suggestions
When VERA finds 3+ free workdays, it places a green suggestion event on your Vera calendar. Leave it = accepted. Delete it = declined.
⚠️
Buffer Day Alerts
If your buffer days have been sitting unused for too long, VERA places a yellow alert on Friday to nudge you.
🎉
Milestone Countdowns
Any calendar event called "Wedding," "Trip," "Concert," etc. gets a purple milestone card with a live countdown.
✈️ The Travel Pipeline — Start to Finish
1
Book your trip → VERA starts tracking
Add the trip to your Life OS calendar and VERA picks it up automatically that night.
2
Email parser reads confirmation emails
Forward flight/hotel confirmations to VERA or let it scan Gmail. It extracts structured itinerary data automatically.
3
48 hours before: Pre-trip briefing
Weather at destination, packing list completion %, flight status, and confirmation numbers — sent automatically via email.
4
During trip: Live flight status
Gate, terminal, delays, and departure time — polled every 15 minutes when your flight is within 24 hours.
5
After return: Memory capture
VERA prompts you to log memories and notes from the trip so they're preserved in your travel history.
Bucket list bonus: If a destination near your trip is on your bucket list, VERA flags it before you leave so you can consider a side trip.

📅 Monthly Cadence & When to Do Nothing — what to expect over time, and what VERA handles without you
🗓 What Happens Each Month
📊
1st of the Month — Monthly Life Review
A structured email recap: goals progress, tasks completed vs overdue, spending summary, PTO burn-rate, and one thing to carry forward into the new month.
🎂
Birthdays & Anniversaries — 30 Days Out
VERA flags important dates 30 days in advance so you have time to plan. Birthdays synced from your calendar automatically appear in the Important Dates tracker.
💳
Bills — As Due Dates Approach
Upcoming bills are flagged as the due date approaches. VERA knows which are on autopay and which need your attention.
🎯
Goal Progress — Ongoing
Goals with a target date that are falling behind get flagged. Goals marked "Doing" are checked for recent activity — if there's been none, VERA nudges you.
💪
Gym Streak — Weekly
VERA tracks your gym attendance. If you miss your typical frequency for a week, it surfaces a gentle nudge — no shame, just a heads-up.

💰 Finance — What VERA Watches
📊
Monthly Spending Trends
VERA reads your transactions and compares this month vs last month by category. Spikes (like +66% dining) get flagged automatically.
💳
Bills & Due Dates
Upcoming bills are tracked by due date. VERA flags ones that aren't on autopay and are approaching. You can also ask "What bills are due this week?" anytime.
🧾
SAT Budget Integration
The household budget (Simple Ass Tracker) feeds into VERA's nightly summary — income, fixed costs, shared expense split, and transfer calculations are all visible via Chat.
📈
Finance Overview Tab
The dashboard Finance tab shows your top spending categories, net vs budget, recent transaction highlights, and any anomalies VERA detected this month.
How to add transactions: VERA reads from your Transactions sheet. Export from Empower (or your bank) in CSV format and paste into the Transactions tab — VERA picks them up the next night.
